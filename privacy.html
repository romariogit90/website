Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Romario Consultante (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we handle when you visit this website or contact us, and how we protect it.

Information we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or third-party scripts, and it does not store any information you type into the contact form. The form simply prepares a message in your own email application; nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

When you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, typically:

  • your name and email address;
  • your company name, if provided;
  • the content of your message and any follow-up correspondence.

Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical data such as IP addresses in server logs for security and operational purposes. See GitHub’s own privacy statement for details.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss a potential engagement.
  • To deliver and administer services you have engaged us for.
  • To meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not use it for unsolicited marketing.

Legal basis

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we process your information on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, to perform a contract, or to comply with legal obligations.

Retention

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within 24 months. Client records are kept for as long as the engagement lasts and thereafter only as long as required by law.

Security and confidentiality

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal and business information. Client information is treated as confidential and shared only with those who need it to carry out the engagement.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to its processing, and to receive a copy of it. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@romarioconsultante.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
